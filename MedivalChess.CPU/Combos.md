# CPU Strategy Reference — Counters and Combos

Use these as soft heuristics, not absolute rules. Prefer actual board evaluation when position, health, objectives, or immediate tactical opportunities conflict with them.

## Counters

### Soldier

**Good against**

* Peasant
* Archer at close range
* Spy
* Engineer
* Bombard at close range
* Cannon at close range

**Bad against**

* Knight
* Crossbowman
* Chariot
* Cannon at range
* Defender wall

---

### Defender

**Good against**

* Soldier
* Peasant
* Low-damage melee
* Chokepoint pressure
* Expensive attackers that would otherwise overkill weaker units

**Bad against**

* Cannon
* Crossbowman
* Ballista
* Bombard when grouped
* Spy-supported heavy attacks

---

### Archer

**Good against**

* Defender
* Peasant
* Spearman
* Engineer
* Spy
* Slow melee without immediate access

**Bad against**

* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Crossbowman
* Catapult
* Princess

---

### Spearman

**Good against**

* Soldier
* Cavalier
* Knight when supported
* Chariot approaching through predictable lines

**Bad against**

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Catapult
* Princess
* Fast flankers approaching outside its attack direction

---

### Peasant

**Good against**

* Heavily damaged enemies
* Expensive single-target attackers through numbers
* Blocking and surrounding

**Bad against**

* Bombard
* Ballista
* Elephant
* Any unit capable of efficiently hitting multiple Peasants

---

### Knight

**Good against**

* Archer
* Spy
* Engineer
* Bombard
* Cannon if it can close
* Catapult if it can close
* Most basic melee units

**Bad against**

* Cannon
* Crossbowman
* Ballista
* Large groups of cheap units
* Spy + high-damage attacker

---

### Crossbowman

**Good against**

* Soldier
* Spearman
* Archer
* Spy
* Bombard
* Other fragile medium-value units

**Bad against**

* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Catapult at long range
* Defender screens protecting melee divers

---

### Cavalier

**Good against**

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Spy
* Engineer
* Bombard
* Cannon
* Catapult
* Injured units that can be attacked without remaining exposed

**Bad against**

* Spearman formations
* Knight in direct combat
* Defender screens
* Dense enemy formations that prevent safe retreat

---

### Chariot

**Good against**

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Spy
* Engineer
* Bombard
* Exposed backline pieces
* Enemies aligned with its movement and attack lanes

**Bad against**

* Units approaching from awkward angles
* Dense blockers
* Spearman formations
* Long-range artillery
* Terrain that restricts lines

---

### Cannon

**Good against**

* Knight
* Defender
* Guard
* Mechanical units
* High-health single targets

**Bad against**

* Knight at close range
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Soldier at close range
* Cheap units where 30 damage causes large overkill

---

### Spy

**Good against**

* High-health targets
* Royals
* Elephant
* Knight
* Guard-protected targets
* Any priority unit that can immediately be hit by a high-damage ally

**Bad against**

* Archer
* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Crossbowman
* Any enemy capable of killing it before the mark is exploited

---

### Catapult

**Good against**

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Cannon
* Ballista
* Bombard
* Static formations
* Units hiding behind terrain or blockers

**Bad against**

* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Fast melee that can close distance
* Units already inside its minimum range

---

### Bombard

**Good against**

* Clustered Peasants
* Clustered Defenders
* Units surrounding a Royal
* Dense formations
* Guard/support clusters

**Bad against**

* Spread formations
* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Crossbowman
* Units inside minimum range

---

### Ox

**Good against**

* Transporting Cannon
* Transporting Catapult
* Transporting Ballista
* Extracting slow or vulnerable units
* Repositioning high-value units

**Bad against**

* Dedicated combat units
* Focus fire
* Attacks that kill the carried unit shortly after delivery

---

### Engineer

**Good against**

* Melee-heavy armies
* Chokepoint pushes
* Predictable movement lanes
* Area denial
* Protecting artillery

**Bad against**

* Archer
* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Catapult
* Enemies that bypass or ignore terrain control

---

### Ballista

**Good against**

* Linear formations
* Multiple enemies aligned behind each other
* Mechanical units
* Defenders protecting valuable backline units
* Large units

**Bad against**

* Spread formations
* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Enemies approaching from multiple angles

---

### Elephant

**Good against**

* Peasant swarms
* Defender walls
* Dense melee formations
* Barricade-heavy areas
* Formations relying on terrain

**Bad against**

* Spy + heavy attacker
* Cannon
* Ballista
* Crossbowman focus fire
* Enemies that can kite or attack from range

---

### Guard

**Good against**

* Protecting Cannon
* Protecting Catapult
* Protecting Ballista
* Protecting Crossbowman
* Protecting Spy
* Protecting Engineer
* Keeping a key damaged unit alive

**Bad against**

* Cannon
* Spy-supported attacks
* AoE
* Situations where the protected unit is low-value

---

### Mercenary

**Good against**

* Archer
* Spy
* Engineer
* Bombard
* Exposed support units
* Immediate tactical pressure in No-Man's-Land

**Bad against**

* Defender
* Knight
* Crossbowman
* Extended fights where upkeep becomes inefficient

---

### Farm

**Good against**

* Long games
* Defensible positions
* Economy-focused strategies

**Bad against**

* Early aggression
* Chariot
* Knight
* Cavalier
* Artillery pressure
* Situations where it cannot survive long enough to repay its cost

---

# Royal Matchups

### King

**Good against**

* Swarms of low-damage units
* Attrition
* Defensive formations
* Defender-heavy armies

**Bad against**

* Cannon
* Ballista
* Spy + high-damage attacker
* Concentrated heavy damage

---

### Princess

**Good against**

* Archer
* Spearman
* Defender
* Units behind friendly screens
* Static formations

**Bad against**

* Knight
* Cavalier
* Chariot
* Long-range artillery

---

### Palace

**Good against**

* Long games
* Defensive positions
* Economy scaling

**Bad against**

* Siege pressure
* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista
* Sustained aggression

---

### Baron

**Good against**

* Peasant swarms
* Soldier formations
* Defender formations
* Dense melee groups
* Any army containing many low-damage units

**Bad against**

* Bombard
* Ballista
* Catapult
* Enemies that force allied units to spread out

---

### Emissary

**Good against**

* Slow armies
* Mechanical units
* Repositioning melee formations
* Escaping threats
* Rapid concentration of force

**Bad against**

* Bombard if allies cluster after moving
* Ballista if repositioning creates lines
* Killing the Emissary before mobility can be exploited

# Strong Combos

## Spy + Cannon

Mark a high-value or high-health target, then hit it with Cannon.

**Especially strong against**

* King
* Elephant
* Knight
* Guard-protected units
* Mechanical units

---

## Spy + Ballista

Use the Spy to amplify a high-value Ballista hit.

Best when the marked target is also positioned so the Ballista can pierce additional enemies.

---

## Spy + Crossbowman

Reliable way to delete medium-health priority targets.

Use when Cannon or Ballista would cause unnecessary overkill.

---

## Defender + Ranged Unit

Place Defender between enemy melee and:

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista
* Bombard

The Defender's purpose is not necessarily to deal damage; it buys additional ranged attacks.

---

## Guard + Artillery

Attach Guard to:

* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista

High priority when the artillery piece is exposed but strategically important.

---

## Guard + Spy

Useful when keeping the Spy alive for another mark is more valuable than protecting a damage dealer.

---

## Ox + Cannon

Use Ox to compensate for Cannon's poor mobility and awkward size.

Strong for:

* setting up firing lanes
* moving between flanks
* escaping melee

---

## Ox + Catapult

Allows rapid repositioning of a normally very slow long-range unit.

Especially useful when the enemy begins closing the Catapult's minimum-range gap.

---

## Ox + Ballista

Reposition Ballista into a new firing line.

Prefer locations where multiple enemies can be pierced.

---

## Engineer + Artillery

Barricades can:

* prevent melee access
* create firing windows
* funnel enemies into lines
* protect minimum-range artillery

Strong with:

* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista
* Bombard

---

## Engineer + Ballista

Use barricades and mines to influence enemy movement into predictable straight lines.

Then exploit those lines with Ballista piercing.

---

## Engineer + Bombard

Use barricades to funnel or cluster enemies.

Bombard then punishes the resulting formation.

---

## Engineer + Catapult

Engineer creates defensive terrain while Catapult attacks over it.

Very strong defensive pairing.

---

## Bombard + Defender

Defenders hold enemies in place while Bombard attacks clustered units.

Avoid friendly-fire damage when possible.

---

## Ballista + Defender

Defenders can stall a line of enemies while Ballista fires through aligned targets.

---

## Cavalier + Ranged Pressure

Use ranged attacks to soften a target, then Cavalier finishes it and retreats.

Good partners:

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Cannon
* Princess

---

## Knight + Archer

Archer pressures slow melee while Knight intercepts anything that closes distance.

---

## Spearman + Archer

Spearman protects the Archer against fast melee approaches while Archer attacks enemies the Spearman cannot safely reach.

---

## Peasant + High-Value Ranged Unit

Use Peasants as cheap blockers around:

* Crossbowman
* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista

Do not cluster them when enemy AoE is present.

---

## Baron + Peasant

Baron's +5 damage massively increases Peasant damage.

High-value synergy due to Peasant's low base attack.

---

## Baron + Defender

Raises Defender attack from weak chip damage to meaningful damage while retaining high durability.

---

## Baron + Soldier

Efficient general-purpose formation with increased damage.

---

## King + Defender

Creates a highly durable frontline.

Good for protecting:

* artillery
* Royals
* objectives
* Farms

---

## King + Guard

Very strong defensive combination for keeping an important allied unit alive.

---

## Princess + Defender

Defenders screen the Princess while she attacks over them.

---

## Emissary + Mechanical Unit

Use Emissary movement effects to compensate for poor Mechanical mobility where legal.

Especially useful with:

* Cannon
* Catapult
* Ballista

---

## Emissary + Melee Formation

Rapidly reposition multiple melee units to:

* surround a target
* reinforce a flank
* escape artillery
* threaten a Royal

---

## Elephant + Ranged Support

Elephant disrupts formations and absorbs attention while ranged units attack from behind.

Good partners:

* Archer
* Crossbowman
* Catapult
* Ballista

---

## Bombard + Elephant

Elephant encourages enemies to cluster or reposition around it; Bombard punishes dense responses.

Avoid hitting the Elephant unnecessarily with friendly splash damage.

---

# Anti-Combos / Situations to Avoid

* Do not cluster cheap units against **Bombard**.
* Do not align valuable units against **Ballista**.
* Do not leave ranged or Mechanical units exposed to **Knight**, **Cavalier**, or **Chariot**.
* Do not waste **Cannon** attacks on 5-HP targets unless the kill is strategically important.
* Do not send **Peasants** into AoE when spreading them achieves the same goal.
* Do not build dense Engineer fortifications that create ideal Bombard targets.
* Do not place valuable units adjacent to a target likely to be hit by Bombard.
* Do not use **Guard** on low-value units while a high-value vulnerable unit needs protection.
* Do not use **Spy marks** on targets that cannot be attacked immediately or soon afterwards.
* Do not expose **Catapult** inside its minimum attack range without protection.
* Do not use **Mercenary** for prolonged low-value fighting when upkeep exceeds its tactical value.
* Do not build **Farm** when expected survival time is too short to recover a meaningful portion of its cost.
